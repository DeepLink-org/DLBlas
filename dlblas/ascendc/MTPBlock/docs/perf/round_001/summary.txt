Bottleneck Analysis:
====================
- ALL kernels are heavily scalar-bound (≥97% scalar time)
- AscendC::Mul, AscendC::Exp, AscendC::Rsqrt contribute < 1% of total time
- Main bottleneck: scalar GetValue/SetValue triple loops in MatMul dot products
- K1 (168ms) and K5 (114ms) dominate due to largest MatMul operations
- K4 (2.3ms) is fastest: simplest computation with no MatMul

Root Cause:
- All MatMul operations use scalar GetValue/SetValue loops instead of SIMD vector ops (AscendC::Mul + ReduceSum) or MatmulImpl
- Weight rows are re-loaded from GM inside inner loops (no UB caching)
- Demo shape (M=8, K=512) is small enough for scalar loops to work functionally but not performantly

Recommended Optimizations (by impact):
1. Replace scalar MatMul with SIMD vector dot product (AscendC::Mul + AscendC::ReduceSum) - 5-20x speedup expected
2. Preload weight matrices into UB (avoid GM re-reads) - 2-5x speedup for K1/K2/K5/K6
3. Integrate MatmulImpl for K1 e_proj/h_proj and K5 w1/w2/w3 - 10-50x speedup
4. Enable Double Buffer for I/O queues (K1, K3, K5, K6) - pipeline overlap
5. Multi-core parallelization on s-dimension - O(cores) speedup
